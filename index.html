ABOUT FOODSHARE

Good food is better when it's shared.

FoodShare is a simple idea: connect people with extra food to people who can use it. Sharing with neighbors can help reduce food waste and build a more caring community.

Our goal is to make it easy to offer food, find nearby donations, and treat one another with kindness.
